# Lalilo

> Lalilo is an adaptive reading and literacy web application for primary-school
> classrooms, founded in Paris in 2016 and acquired by Renaissance (Wisconsin
> Rapids, USA) in 2021. It delivers roughly 90,000 interactive phonics, fluency
> and comprehension exercises that adapt in real time to each student, uses
> speech recognition to assess oral reading, and gives teachers a dashboard for
> differentiated small-group instruction. It is used by around 40,000 teachers
> and 400,000 students a year in France (cycles 2–3), with explicit support for
> DYS, allophone, ULIS, SEGPA and IME learners.

## Status

Lalilo publishes **no public API, no developer portal and no API
documentation**. As of 2026-07-19 no `api.lalilo.com`, `developer.lalilo.com`
or `docs.lalilo.com` host resolves, and no OpenAPI, AsyncAPI, GraphQL schema or
webhook catalog is published. The product is consumed through the teacher and
student web apps at https://app.lalilo.com/. Integration and rostering
questions are handled through Renaissance support rather than a self-serve
developer program.
